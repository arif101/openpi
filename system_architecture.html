What's novel — and why it's "general, not a lookup table"

Prior VLAs (π0.5) bake object identity & position into their weights → they memorize and collapse when an object moves (swap: 100% → 17%). The field "fixes" this with more data (a bigger lookup table) or test-time steering. We make position-invariance structural: an SE(2)-equivariant motor produces correspondingly-rotated actions when the object's bearing changes, so success-vs-displacement is flat by construction — the policy cannot encode "object X lives at position P." Validated by deep research (104 agents, 24/25 claims confirmed): this wedge — equivariance as the cure for VLA counterfactual collapse — is unclaimed (ISP/EquiBot/EquiDiff exist but none evaluated on LIBERO-PRO/counterfactual robustness).

Do we have our own foundation-model VLA?

Partly — and the part that's ours is the part that matters. It is a factored VLA, not a monolithic from-scratch foundation model: the binder is frozen web-pretrained DINOv2, and the motor borrows π0.5's competence by distillation. But the architecture and the load-bearing idea are ours: the equivariant/canonicalized motor that is position-general by construction is novel, validated, and SOTA on the hardest LIBERO-PRO axis — π0 is nowhere at test time. So: we own a novel VLA architecture with a SOTA capability; we do not (yet) own a from-scratch pretrained foundation model — and the research shows we shouldn't try to out-scale π0 (10k-hr data moat); our edge is the factoring + equivariance.

Capability map — what it's good at, weak at, and the future

✅ Strong (validated)

  • Position / relocation generalization — SOTA swap 0.76–0.80, by construction not memorization.
  • Distractor robustness — motor never sees the global scene; clutter can't confuse it.
  • Precise grasping — wrist-cam visual precision distilled from π0.5.
  • Open-vocab identity binding — DINOv2 30/30 on relocated look-alikes.

⚠️ Weak / in progress

  • Precise 3D localization — depth unprojection bug (21cm err); the perception front-end is the bottleneck, not the motor.
  • Goal-following, not goal-correcting — needs an accurate goal; large binder error breaks it.
  • Only swap axis deep-tested — object/task/lan/environment axes pending.
  • SE(2) proxy, not the full escnn/ISP equivariant net yet.

🧠 Future — capabilities & metacognition

  • Metacognition: a self-model that knows when it's wrong (ε prediction-error monitor we already prototyped: AUROC 0.97) → fail-detect, retry, ask-for-help.
  • Goal-correcting closed-loop motor (RL / ISP-style) — robust to imperfect binding.
  • Stronger perception: LocateAnything detector + robust depth → fix the e2e bottleneck.
  • Full equivariant net + all LIBERO-PRO axes → the paper.
  • World-model reasoning + deliberation (think-reflect-revise) layer over the factored core.
Pipeline: binder (DINOv2/LocateAnything) → 3D goal (depth) → SE(2)-canon motor → robot. Novel component = ③. Metacognition (the ε self-model, surprise-gated memory, know-when-uncertain) is the natural next layer that turns a competent VLA into a deliberative one.
